# Refund Policy

**Effective Date**: January 1, 2025  
**Last Updated**: January 8, 2025

## Our Commitment to Customer Satisfaction

At PetArt AI, we are committed to providing high-quality AI-powered pet portrait generation services. We understand that sometimes our service may not meet your expectations, and we have established this refund policy to ensure fair treatment for all customers.

## 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We offer a **30-day money-back guarantee** on all purchases. If you are not satisfied with our service for any reason, you may request a full refund within 30 days of your purchase date.

### How to Request a Refund

1. **Email us** at support@petart.ai within 30 days of purchase
2. **Include** your order number and reason for the refund request
3. **Allow** 3-5 business days for processing

## Refund Eligibility

### Eligible for Refund

You are eligible for a refund if:

- You request it within 30 days of purchase
- The service was unavailable for more than 48 consecutive hours
- Technical issues prevented you from using the service
- Generated content quality is significantly below advertised standards
- You were charged incorrectly or for services not received

### Not Eligible for Refund

Refunds are not available for:

- Requests made after 30 days from purchase date
- Credits that have been fully used
- Dissatisfaction with artistic style (subjective preferences)
- Violation of our Terms of Service resulting in account suspension
- Chargebacks initiated without first contacting our support team

## Partial Refunds

In some cases, we may offer partial refunds:

- If you've used a portion of your credits, we may refund the unused portion
- For annual subscriptions canceled within the first 30 days, we'll refund the difference between the annual price and monthly price for months used
- Technical issues affecting partial service functionality

## Processing Time

### Refund Timeline

- **Review Period**: 3-5 business days to review your request
- **Approval Notification**: Email confirmation within 24 hours of decision
- **Processing Time**: 5-10 business days for funds to appear in your account
- **Payment Method**: Refunds are issued to the original payment method

## Special Circumstances

### Technical Issues

If you experience technical problems:

1. First, contact our support team for assistance
2. We'll attempt to resolve the issue within 48 hours
3. If unresolved, you're eligible for a full refund

### Quality Concerns

For concerns about generated portrait quality:

1. Provide examples of unsatisfactory results
2. Our team will review and may offer:
   - Additional credits for regeneration
   - Technical support to improve results
   - Full or partial refund if quality standards aren't met

## Subscription Cancellations

### Monthly Subscriptions

- Cancel anytime through your account dashboard
- No refund for the current billing period
- Service continues until the end of the billing period

### Annual Subscriptions

- Cancel within 30 days for a full refund
- After 30 days, no refund but service continues until subscription end
- Downgrade to monthly available with pro-rated credit

## Credit Expiration

- Purchased credits expire 12 months from purchase date
- Expired credits are not eligible for refund
- We'll send reminder emails before expiration

## Dispute Resolution

If you're unsatisfied with our refund decision:

1. **Appeal Process**: Email legal@petart.ai with additional information
2. **Response Time**: We'll review appeals within 7 business days
3. **Final Decision**: Our review team's decision is final

## Chargebacks

Before initiating a chargeback with your payment provider:

- Please contact our support team first
- Chargebacks may result in account suspension
- We reserve the right to dispute unwarranted chargebacks
