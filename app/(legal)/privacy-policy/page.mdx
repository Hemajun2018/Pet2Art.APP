## 1. Introduction

At Pet2Art, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our AI-powered pet portrait generation service at pet2art.app ("the Service").

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide and improve our Service:

### Account Information
- **What we collect**: Name, email address, and authentication credentials
- **Purpose**: Account management, authentication, and customer support

### Content Data  
- **What we collect**: Pet photos you upload and AI-generated portraits
- **Purpose**: Providing our core service - transforming your pet photos into art
- **Retention**: Photos stored for 30 days, portraits until you delete them

### Usage Data
- **What we collect**: Feature usage, style preferences, device information
- **Purpose**: Improving AI models and enhancing user experience
- **Retention**: Aggregated and anonymized after 90 days

### Payment Information
- **Processing**: Handled securely by Creem.io (PCI-DSS compliant)
- **What we store**: Transaction IDs and purchase history only
- **What we don't store**: Credit card numbers or sensitive payment details

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

- **Service Delivery**: Generate and deliver your pet portraits
- **Account Management**: Manage your credits, subscriptions, and preferences
- **Improvements**: Enhance our AI models and develop new artistic styles
- **Communications**: Send service updates, receipts, and support responses
- **Legal Compliance**: Meet regulatory and legal requirements
- **Security**: Protect against fraud and unauthorized access

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

### We DO NOT sell your personal information

We only share your data with:

- **Service Providers**
  - Creem.io for payment processing
  - Cloud infrastructure providers for hosting
  - Analytics services (anonymized data only)

- **Legal Requirements**
  - When required by law or court order
  - To protect rights, property, or safety
  - To investigate fraud or security issues

- **Business Transfers**
  - In case of merger, acquisition, or sale
  - You'll be notified before any transfer

## 5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

- **Encryption**: TLS/SSL for data in transit, AES-256 for data at rest
- **Access Control**: Role-based access with multi-factor authentication
- **Infrastructure**: Secure cloud hosting with regular security updates
- **Monitoring**: 24/7 security monitoring and intrusion detection
- **Audits**: Regular third-party security assessments
- **Payment Security**: All payments processed through Creem.io's PCI-DSS compliant platform

## 6. Data Retention

| Data Type | Retention Period | Reason |
|-----------|------------------|---------|
| Account Information | Account lifetime + 30 days | Service continuity |
| Uploaded Pet Photos | 30 days after processing | Processing buffer |
| Generated Portraits | Until deleted by user | User ownership |
| Payment Records | 7 years | Legal requirements |
| Support Communications | 2 years | Service improvement |
| Usage Analytics | 90 days (then anonymized) | Performance monitoring |

## 7. Your Privacy Rights

### For All Users
- **Access**: Request a copy of your personal data
- **Correction**: Update inaccurate information
- **Deletion**: Request removal of your data
- **Portability**: Receive your data in a portable format
- **Objection**: Opt-out of certain data processing

### GDPR Rights (European Users)
- Right to be informed about data collection
- Right to restrict processing
- Right to withdraw consent at any time
- Right to lodge a complaint with supervisory authority

### CCPA Rights (California Users)
- Right to know what personal information is collected
- Right to know if information is sold or disclosed
- Right to say no to the sale of personal information
- Right to equal service and price

## 8. Cookies and Tracking

We use minimal cookies for:

- **Essential Cookies**: Required for site functionality
- **Analytics Cookies**: Understanding usage patterns (anonymized)
- **Preference Cookies**: Remembering your settings

You can control cookies through your browser settings.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Pet2Art is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected data from a child, please contact us immediately.

## 10. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers, including:

- Standard contractual clauses
- Adequacy decisions
- Privacy Shield frameworks (where applicable)

## 11. Changes to This Policy

Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
