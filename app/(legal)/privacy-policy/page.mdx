## 1. Introduction

Pet2Art at pet2art.app provides AI pet-art generation with a privacy-minimal approach.

## 2. What We Don't Do

- Do not sell your personal data
- No cross-site ad tracking
- Do not process/store full card data (payments via Creem)
- Do not use your content for external model training or marketing unless you explicitly opt in

## 3. What We Process

- **Account & Contact**: email, display name (if any), account settings
- **Payments & Billing**: processed by Creem; we only receive transaction ID, plan, amount, billing status, tax info (if applicable)
- **User Content**: pet photos you upload, prompts, outputs, metadata
- **Usage Data**: device/browser, IP, language, clicks, error logs; for basic analytics & security
- **Cookies/Local Storage**: session and preference cookies only

## 4. How We Use It

- Deliver and maintain the Service (generation, downloads, history, billing/credits)
- Process payments, prevent fraud/abuse
- Support and notify about important changes
- Aggregate/de-identified analytics
- Legal compliance

## 5. Sharing & Disclosure

Only to contracted providers: Creem (payments), cloud/CDN, logging/analytics; or when required by law/safety. No selling of data.

## 6. International Transfers

Where applicable, safeguarded via Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

## 7. Retention

- **Uploads**: up to 30 days (or user deletion)
- **Outputs**: until you delete
- **Logs**: typically ≤ 12 months
- **Billing/tax records**: up to 7 years or longer if required

## 8. Your Rights

Access, correction, deletion, restriction/objection, portability, withdrawal of consent (no impact on prior processing). Contact: petart_studio@outlook.com.

## 9. Security

Reasonable technical/organizational measures; no system is 100% secure.

## 10. Children

Not directed to under-13s.

## 11. Changes

We may update and post the new date; material changes will be notified in-product or via email.
