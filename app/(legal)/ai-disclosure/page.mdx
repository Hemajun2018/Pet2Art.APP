## About Our AI Platform

Pet2Art is an independent AI wrapper service that provides a user-friendly interface for transforming pet photos into artistic portraits. We want to be completely transparent about how our technology works.

## Technology Stack

Our platform offers a user-friendly interface built on top of advanced AI models including:

- **GPT-4 Vision** by OpenAI for image understanding and generation
- Custom image processing algorithms for style transfer
- Proprietary optimization techniques for pet-specific enhancements

## Important Disclaimers

### Independence Statement
**This platform is an independent product and is not affiliated with OpenAI, Google, or any other AI model providers.** We provide access to these models through our custom interface and added features.

### Service Nature
We are an AI wrapper service, which means:
- We provide a simplified, user-friendly interface to complex AI models
- We add custom features and optimizations specifically for pet portraits
- We handle all the technical complexity so you can focus on creating beautiful art
- We are responsible for customer support and service quality

## Model Capabilities and Limitations

### What Our AI Can Do
- Transform pet photos into various artistic styles
- Maintain pet's unique features and characteristics
- Generate high-resolution images suitable for printing
- Process multiple art styles quickly

### Current Limitations
- **Only Pet Photos**: Our service is optimized exclusively for pet portraits. Human photos are not supported.
- **Photo Quality Dependency**: Results depend on input photo quality - clear, well-lit photos work best
- **Style Accuracy**: While we strive for consistency, artistic styles may vary slightly between generations
- **Processing Time**: Complex requests may take 2-3 minutes to complete
- **No Video Support**: Currently, we only support static images, not video content

## Data Processing

### How We Use AI Models
1. Your pet photo is securely uploaded to our servers
2. We process it through our optimization pipeline
3. We send it to AI models with our custom prompts
4. The generated art is returned and post-processed
5. Final artwork is delivered to you

### Privacy and Security
- We never share your photos with third parties except for processing
- Photos are deleted from AI provider servers immediately after processing
- We don't use your photos to train AI models
- All data transmission is encrypted
